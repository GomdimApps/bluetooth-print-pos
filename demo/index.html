Scales both text wrapping and the image/barcode width ceiling — applies to Print, Preview and Test Print below.

Preview renders exactly what would be printed — same text wrapping, same image dithering, real scannable Code128/ITF/QR — with no printer and no Bluetooth needed at all. If a barcode is too wide for the paper, the preview shows it clipped with a red warning instead of finding out from the printer's own "wide error!" on real paper.
